EXPERIMENT 2 

Synthesis of  Copper Oxide Nanoparticles by Solgel method .

Aim:
    To Synthesis Copper Oxode Nanoparticles by Solgel method.
  
Materials required:

   Chemicals required:
   
 1.         Precursor-Copper sulphate
 2.         Solvent-Distilled water
 3. Reducing Agent -Sodium hydroxide
 4. Surfactant     -Sodium Dodecyl Sulphate

    Apparatus Required :
1.           Beaker
2.         conical flask
3.     Measuring cylinder
4.     Magnetic Stirrer with hot plate
5.          Burette
6.         Thermometer
7.     PH paper
   
Procedure:

1.    Dissolve copper nitrate in distilled water to form a clear blue solution.
2.    Stir the mixture continuously at room temperature or slightly heated
3.    Continue heating and stirring until the solution becomes thick and forms a gel.
4.    Dry the gel at ~100–120°C in an oven.
5.    This removes water and forms a dry precursor.
6.    Grind the black powder to obtain fine Copper Oxide nanoparticles.
